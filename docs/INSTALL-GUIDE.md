# Getting SupplyLine on your devices

Your administrator sends you an invitation email. You never need a password: enter your
email and SupplyLine emails you a 6-digit code.

## In a browser (any computer)
Open the SupplyLine address from your invitation, enter your email, then the code.

## Windows PC app
1. Download the SupplyLine installer from the link your administrator gives you and run it.
2. Open SupplyLine from the Start menu and sign in.
3. It updates itself: when a new version is ready you'll see “Restart to update”.

## iPhone / iPad
- From the App Store: search “SupplyLine” (when published), install, sign in.
- Or install it from Safari: open the SupplyLine address › Share › **Add to Home Screen**.
  To get alerts: open it from the Home Screen › Account › turn on push notifications.

## Android phone / tablet
- From Google Play: search “SupplyLine” (when published), install, sign in.
- Or install it from Chrome: open the SupplyLine address › ⋮ › **Install app**.
  Turn on alerts under Account › push notifications.

## Good to know
- **No signal?** Screens you've opened before still show (read-only) with an “offline” banner.
  Saving needs a connection.
- **Alerts:** the 🔔 at the top shows everything meant for you; push notifications are optional.
- **Search:** the search box (or ⌕ on a phone) finds jobs, lists (“2249 list 3”), POs, BOLs, people, units.
- **Help:** ask your administrator, or email the support address on the sign-in page.

## For administrators: network folders
If you file documents to your own network drive while SupplyLine is hosted online, install the
**SupplyLine Sync** app on one office PC — see `apps/sync/README.md` (about 5 minutes).
